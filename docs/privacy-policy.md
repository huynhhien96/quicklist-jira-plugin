# Privacy Policy for Quick List

**Effective date:** June 29, 2026
**Last updated:** June 29, 2026

## 1. Overview

Quick List ("the App") is a Jira issue panel app built on the Atlassian Forge platform. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is stored, how long it is retained, and the rights available to users.

By installing or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 3. Data Collected

Quick List collects only the data that users explicitly enter within the App:

- Task summaries
- Task statuses (To Do / Done)
- Task notes

The App does not collect:

- Personal data or user identifiers
- Names, email addresses, or account information
- IP addresses or device information
- Usage analytics or behavioral data

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

The App processes data solely on the basis of **legitimate interest** — to provide the core functionality of the App as requested by the user. No data is processed beyond what is necessary to deliver this functionality.

## 5. Data Storage

All data is stored exclusively within **Atlassian Forge Storage**, infrastructure managed and operated by Atlassian. Data is scoped per Jira issue and is accessible only within the context of that specific issue.

The App does not transmit any data to external servers or third-party services. No data leaves the Atlassian platform.

## 6. Data Security

The App relies on Atlassian's built-in security controls for data protection, including encryption at rest and in transit, access control, and platform-level compliance certifications. The App itself introduces no additional data transfer or external exposure.

## 7. Data Retention

Stored data is automatically deleted after **90 days of inactivity** — defined as 90 consecutive days without any task being saved in the App for the associated Jira issue. Users may also remove individual tasks or all tasks at any time using the controls within the panel.

## 8. Data Sharing

Quick List does not share any data with third parties. All data remains within the Atlassian platform and is subject to your organization's Atlassian data governance policies.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union). The App does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has submitted data through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## 10. User Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights regarding your data:

- **Right of access** - request a copy of the data stored by the App
- **Right to erasure** - request deletion of your data at any time
- **Right to rectification** - request correction of inaccurate data
- **Right to data portability** - request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to object** - object to the processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address below. For complete data removal from Atlassian Forge Storage, you may also contact your Atlassian site administrator.
